    Missable trophies

    Built from the official descriptions and checked against PowerPyx, GTABase and Push Square: only one trophy may be missable, and it is easy to protect.

    Things that look missable but are not

    • All 12 story trophies — automatic, none can be missed; chapter trophies may pop a little late, after the first mission of the next chapter.
    • Kenton’s strand — he goes quiet after Chapter 1 and returns in Chapters 2–3; all 7 shipwrecks stay doable.
    • Optional missions that disappear — there are several hundred, and 100% needs any 100, so a few expiring ones do not matter.
    • Quest givers who go away mid-story — every trophy-related giver comes back and stays after the story.
    • Fences that close for good — none of them has a trophy quest; new fences unlock as you go.
    • Magazine collectibles in story buildings — they also spawn elsewhere, and no trophy needs all of them.
    • Hourglass (timed) quests — none is tied to a trophy.
    • Your criminal profile — Irredeemable is permanent and makes police more aggressive, but it locks out no trophy; still, avoid it.

    Save advice from PowerPyx: 15 manual save slots + 1 autosave, no mission replay — keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), and rotate the other 5 during free-roam cleanup. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off your platinum file.

    How the tracker works

    • Tick a trophy and it is saved in this browser (localStorage) — close the page and your progress is still here.
    • Export progress downloads your ticks as a JSON file; Import progress reads one back.
    • Hide story spoilers masks the names and descriptions of story trophies you have not earned yet; ticking one reveals it.
    • Filters: by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, side, crime, exploration — and online, which this game has none of), by grade, by earned state, and by search.
    • This app is fully offline: it loads only its own files and makes no network request. Source links open only if you click them.
